What it does

Everything you need to understand your skin.

AI Skin Scan

Point your camera at any skin concern. YOLOv11 runs entirely on-device, drawing real bounding boxes with condition, confidence, and severity.

Progress Tracker

Trends per tracked spot, so two unrelated warts never merge into one timeline. Every scan keeps its photo and editable notes — tap any entry to revisit the full result.

Clinic Locator

GPS-powered map finds nearby dermatology clinics via Google Places, with driving routes and ETAs. Users can confirm whether a clinic is still open, and permanently closed places are filtered out.

Research Contribution

After saving a scan, opt in to share it anonymously over Wi-Fi. You choose per scan — it is never a silent side effect of saving.

Accessibility

Font size slider and high contrast mode propagate across every screen — manually verified across all conditions and UI states.

Process

Three steps to a result.

01

Capture

Use your camera with pinch-to-zoom, or pick from gallery. Position the affected area inside the guide frame.

02

Analyze

YOLOv11 runs on-device in seconds. Bounding boxes appear with condition name, confidence score, and severity.

03

Act

Save to Progress, find a nearby clinic, or contribute anonymously to the research dataset.

AI Model

YOLOv11, trained on six conditions.

The v2 model is a merged 6-class YOLOv11-small, exported to TFLite and verified live on-device. Instance-aware oversampling eliminated the v1 model's cross-condition confusion — the off-diagonal ceiling is now just 0.09.
